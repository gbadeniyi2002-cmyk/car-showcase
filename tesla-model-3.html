Introduction

The Tesla Model 3 is a fully electric midsize sedan designed for efficient driving, software-led features and quick acceleration. It does not use a traditional petrol engine.

History

The Model 3 was unveiled in 2016, with customer deliveries beginning in 2017. It became one of the best-known electric cars worldwide.

Engine and Performance

Instead of a petrol engine, the Model 3 uses electric motors and a rechargeable battery pack. Range and acceleration depend on the battery, motor configuration, model year and driving conditions.

Interior Features

The cabin has a minimalist layout centred around a large touchscreen, with limited physical controls and software-based vehicle functions. It also provides seating for five people.

Exterior Design

Its smooth body shape, enclosed front styling and aerodynamic profile help reduce air resistance. The simple design gives the car a modern appearance.

Specifications

Body style: electric sedan. Seating capacity: 5. Powertrain: battery-electric. Drivetrain: rear-wheel or all-wheel drive depending on version. It uses a rechargeable battery instead of petrol or diesel.

Interesting Facts

The Model 3 receives many feature improvements through over-the-air software updates. This allows some vehicle functions to be improved without visiting a workshop.

Conclusion

The Tesla Model 3 demonstrates how electric power and software can be combined in a practical everyday sedan. It is an important model in the development of electric vehicles.
