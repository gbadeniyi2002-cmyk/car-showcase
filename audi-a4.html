Introduction

The Audi A4 is a premium compact executive car known for understated styling, a comfortable cabin and available quattro all-wheel drive.

History

The A4 nameplate arrived in 1994 as the successor to the Audi 80. It has been produced across several generations and has become an important model in Audi's range.

Engine and Performance

Depending on generation, the A4 has used turbocharged petrol and diesel engines, with manual or automatic transmissions and front-wheel or quattro all-wheel drive.

Interior Features

The interior commonly includes a clean dashboard layout, quality materials, comfortable seating and an infotainment system. Some versions also include digital instrument displays and driver-assistance features.

Exterior Design

The A4 uses a restrained design with a wide front grille, precise body lines and modern headlamp styling.

Specifications

Body style: compact executive sedan or Avant wagon. Seating capacity: usually 5. Drivetrain: front-wheel or quattro all-wheel drive depending on model. Engine and transmission vary by year and trim.

Interesting Facts

Audi's quattro all-wheel-drive system is a well-known feature associated with many A4 versions. It is designed to improve traction in different driving conditions.

Conclusion

The Audi A4 offers a blend of comfort, technology and confident road manners. It is a practical option for drivers interested in a premium executive car.
