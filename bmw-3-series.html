Introduction

The BMW 3 Series is a compact executive car recognised for its balance of comfort, technology and engaging handling. It is designed for both everyday driving and performance.

History

Introduced in 1975, the BMW 3 Series developed through multiple generations and became one of BMW's best-known model families.

Engine and Performance

Engine choices vary by generation and include four-cylinder and six-cylinder petrol or diesel engines, plus plug-in hybrid options in selected versions. Rear-wheel drive and all-wheel drive are available depending on trim.

Interior Features

The cabin focuses on driver comfort and ergonomics. Features may include supportive seats, a central infotainment display, digital instruments and modern connectivity.

Exterior Design

Its design typically features BMW's kidney grille, athletic proportions, sculpted sides and distinctive lighting.

Specifications

Body style: compact executive sedan or wagon in selected markets. Seating capacity: usually 5. Drivetrain: rear-wheel or all-wheel drive depending on version. Engine and transmission options vary by model year.

Interesting Facts

The BMW 3 Series has been offered in several body styles, including sedan, touring wagon, coupe and convertible in different generations.

Conclusion

The BMW 3 Series remains a popular choice for drivers seeking a premium car with sporty handling, comfort and everyday practicality.
