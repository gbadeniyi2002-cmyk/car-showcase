Introduction

The Mercedes-Benz C-Class is a compact luxury car known for its refined comfort, modern technology and elegant styling. It combines performance with everyday practicality.

History

The C-Class was introduced in 1993 as the successor to the Mercedes-Benz 190. Over several generations, it has developed into one of the company's most popular models.

Engine and Performance

Depending on the model year and trim, the C-Class is offered with turbocharged petrol or diesel engines, hybrid assistance and automatic transmissions. Performance ranges from economical daily driving to sporty AMG versions.

Interior Features

The cabin commonly features supportive seats, quality materials, digital displays, climate control and smartphone connectivity. Higher trims may include additional luxury and entertainment features.

Exterior Design

Its exterior design combines a distinctive grille, sculpted body panels, LED lighting and a balanced luxury-sedan profile.

Specifications

Body style: luxury compact sedan. Seating capacity: usually 5. Drivetrain: varies by version. Transmission: automatic or manual in selected markets. Engine options depend on model year and trim.

Interesting Facts

The C-Class name replaced the 190 model line. Its AMG versions provide substantially higher performance than standard trims.

Conclusion

The Mercedes-Benz C-Class combines comfort, technology and performance in a practical premium sedan. It remains a popular choice among luxury-car enthusiasts.
