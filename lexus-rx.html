Introduction

The Lexus RX is a midsize luxury SUV designed around comfort, refinement, safety and premium features. It is suitable for families and drivers who appreciate luxury.

History

The RX was introduced in 1998 and helped popularise the luxury crossover SUV segment. It has developed through several generations with improvements in design, technology and performance.

Engine and Performance

RX powertrains vary by generation and market, including V6 petrol, turbocharged four-cylinder, hybrid and plug-in hybrid systems. Power and fuel economy depend on the version.

Interior Features

The cabin typically offers comfortable seating, careful sound insulation, premium materials and a large infotainment display. Some models also provide advanced safety and driver-assistance features.

Exterior Design

Its exterior is characterised by a bold grille, flowing bodywork, high ride height and distinctive lighting. The design gives the vehicle a modern luxury appearance.

Specifications

Body style: midsize luxury SUV. Seating capacity: commonly 5. Powertrain: petrol, hybrid or plug-in hybrid depending on version. Drivetrain: front-wheel or all-wheel drive depending on model.

Interesting Facts

The Lexus RX was among the early luxury crossovers to combine SUV practicality with car-like comfort. It helped establish the popularity of luxury SUVs.

Conclusion

The Lexus RX is suited to drivers who value a quiet, comfortable and well-equipped luxury SUV. Its combination of space and refinement makes it a popular premium vehicle.
